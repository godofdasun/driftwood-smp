About the server

Driftwood SMP started as a group chat idea between six creators who kept guesting on each other's videos and figured a shared world would be more fun than another one-off collab. There's no theme forced onto anyone — some members are here to build, some are here to blow things up, and the map has room for both.

The world is whitelisted and private, but every episode made on it is public. Think of this site as the atlas for the atlas: a map of who's playing, what they're building, and where to watch it unfold.
